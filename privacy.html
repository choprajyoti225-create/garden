Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

The short version

Keepers of the Light is a single informational page. It has no accounts, no forms, no comments, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not set cookies and do not ask you for any personal information.

Information collected automatically

Like almost every website, this one is delivered by third-party services that receive basic technical information whenever your browser requests a page:

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Netlify. When you visit, Netlify's servers may process standard request data such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request, in order to deliver the site, keep it secure, and diagnose problems. See Privacy Policy.
  • Fonts. The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers and shares your IP address and browser information with Google. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

We do not use this information to identify you, build a profile of you, or sell or share it with anyone else.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies of its own, and it does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

This site is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information about you, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over data processed by the hosting and font providers named above; please refer to their privacy policies for how to exercise them.

Changes to this policy

If the way this site works changes — for example, if analytics or a contact form are added — this policy will be updated and the date at the top of the page revised.
